Questions we get asked.

How much does IT support cost?

It depends on the size of your team, the number of devices and the systems you use. Send us a few details and we reply with a clear quote, with no surprise charges.

How quickly will you respond?

We reply to every message within one business day. Clients on the Business plan get priority response.

Do you work remotely or on-site?

Most problems are fixed remotely and quickly. When something needs hands on the hardware, we visit you. We are based in Tembisa.

What devices and systems do you support?

Windows and Mac computers, phones, printers, networks, email and common cloud services such as Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace.

Are we locked in to a contract?

No. Everything we set up is documented and handed over properly, so you can take it elsewhere whenever you want.

What should I do if we think we have been hacked?

Disconnect the affected device from the internet, do not delete anything, and contact us straight away. We will contain the problem, recover your data and secure your systems.

Can you build a website or app for my business?

Yes. We build websites, internal tools and automations, and keep looking after them once they are live. We agree the scope and price before we start.

Describe the problem or the project. We reply within one business day with next steps.
